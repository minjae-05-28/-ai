# lit_symbiont_at_bias

> Reduced symbiont genomes drift to AT-rich codons, enriching F, Y, M, I, N, K and raising protein pI.

**출처**: [Moran 1996 PNAS; McCutcheon & Moran 2012, Nature Reviews Microbiology](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrmicro2670)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
14 insect endosymbionts and E. coli: proteome size vs FYMINK Spearman -0.65, vs median pI -0.72 (E. coli FYMINK 0.24, pI 6.6; Buchnera 0.40-0.43, pI ~10).
