# lit_nitrogen_economy

> Microbes in nitrogen-poor oceans use amino acids with fewer side-chain nitrogen atoms.

**출처**: [Grzymski & Dussaq 2012, ISME Journal](https://www.nature.com/articles/ismej201193)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Side-chain N per residue falls in 5 of 6 oligotroph pairs: Synechococcus elongatus -> Prochlorococcus marinus -0.026, Cereibacter sphaeroides -> Candidatus Pelagibacter ubique -0.036, Sphingobium japonicum -> Sphingopyxis alaskensis -0.007, Nitrososphaera viennensis -> Nitrosopumilus maritimus -0.029, Synechococcus elongatus -> Synechococcus sp. WH 8102 +0.008, Cupriavidus necator -> Polynucleobacter asymbioticus -0.038.
