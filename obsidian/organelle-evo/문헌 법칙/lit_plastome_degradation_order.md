# lit_plastome_degradation_order

> In plants turning parasitic the plastome degrades in stages: ndh, then photosynthesis and plastid RNA polymerase, then ATP synthase, then housekeeping genes.

**출처**: [Wicke & Naumann 2018; Graham et al. 2017, New Phytologist](https://nph.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/nph.14398)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent (one lineage)

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Epifagus vs tobacco: ndh 100%, photosynthesis+PEP 100%, ATP synthase 100%, housekeeping 23% lost. Monotone with stage. One lineage only; the plastid co-loss modules (organelle_modules_v1) also put ndh genes in one block.
