# lit_hydrophobicity

> Hydrophobic, membrane-embedded proteins are retained in organelle genomes because they are hard to import.

**출처**: [von Heijne 1986; Bjorkholm et al. 2015](https://www.news-medical.net/news/20220916/Researchers-identify-universal-features-shaping-organelle-gene-retention.aspx)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: partly

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Mitochondria: hydrophobicity loss weight -0.32 and TM helices -0.37 (both retained, CIs exclude 0). Plastids: no effect (CIs include 0). Insect endosymbionts, which import nothing: membrane proteins are lost more (+0.16).
