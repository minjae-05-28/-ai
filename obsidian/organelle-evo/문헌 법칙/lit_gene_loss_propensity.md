# lit_gene_loss_propensity

> Genes with low dispensability and high conservation have a low propensity to be lost (propensity for gene loss, PGL).

**출처**: [Krylov, Wolf, Rogozin & Koonin 2003, Genome Research 13:2229](https://genome.cshlp.org/content/13/10/2229)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Family loss rate in 79 parasite pairs vs ubiquity in free-living eukaryotes: Spearman -0.58 over 8416 families; least-ubiquitous quartile loses 68%, most-ubiquitous 24%.
