# lit_scaling_laws

> Genes per functional category scale as a power of total gene number: transcription regulators and signalling ~N^2, metabolism ~N^1, translation ~N^0.

**출처**: [van Nimwegen 2003, Trends in Genetics](https://arxiv.org/html/physics/0307001v1)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent (metabolism exponent lower: 0.7)

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Across 36 bacteria and archaea: transcription regulation exponent 1.95 [1.63, 2.27], signalling 2.47 [1.80, 3.36], metabolism 0.70 [0.53, 0.89], translation 0.01 [-0.02, 0.04].
