# lit_black_queen

> Free-living bacteria lose 'leaky' functions that the community supplies, e.g. hydrogen-peroxide removal in Prochlorococcus.

**출처**: [Morris, Lenski & Zinser 2012, mBio 3:e00036-12](https://journals.asm.org/doi/pdf/10.1128/mbio.00036-12)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Synechococcus elongatus 2 catalase/peroxidase genes -> Prochlorococcus marinus 0; Cereibacter sphaeroides 3 catalase/peroxidase genes -> Candidatus Pelagibacter ubique 0.
