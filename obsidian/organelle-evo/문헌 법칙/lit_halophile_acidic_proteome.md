# lit_halophile_acidic_proteome

> 'Salt-in' halophiles have acidic proteomes (excess Asp and Glu, low pI).

**출처**: [Reviewed in Oren 2008, Saline Systems; e.g. Paul et al. 2008, Genome Biology](https://genomebiology.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/gb-2008-9-4-r70)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Across species, acidic excess vs optimal NaCl Spearman 0.58, median pI -0.52; in relative->extremophile pairs a 10% NaCl rise adds +0.022 acidic excess.
