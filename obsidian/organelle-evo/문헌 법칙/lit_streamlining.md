# lit_streamlining

> Oligotrophs evolve small genomes with few regulators (selection for nutrient economy).

**출처**: [Giovannoni, Thrash & Temperton 2014, ISME Journal 8:1553](https://www.nature.com/articles/ismej201460)  
**우리 데이터 판정**: consistent

## 우리 데이터로 다시 시험한 결과
Median genes 1598 (oligotrophs) vs 3327 (others); transcription regulators per 1000 genes 20 vs 60. Oligotrophy is the only environment axis that raises the share of families lost (severity_environment_v1, +0.80).
