# convergent_expansion_v1

> [!abstract] 적용 범위
> Gene-family copy-number expansion in eukaryotic parasites, across independent lineages.

**모형** — Family expanded in a pair: parasite copies >= 2x and >= ancestor + 3. Convergent: expanded in more clades than a family-wise 95% permutation threshold.
