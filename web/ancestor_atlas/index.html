    How to read it, and its limits

    Grades come from how confident each call is. The agreement rates come from checking calls of the same kind against thousands of gene trees (that check used an earlier 250-species tree).

    A cell's shade is the share of that function's gene families the ancestor had. Each function also includes families that only exist in some lineages (for example animal sperm proteins), so a pale cell does not mean the function itself was missing. Tap a cell to check the families one by one.

    Genes that moved from plastids into the nucleus can make deep ancestors look photosynthetic. For families concentrated in plastid-bearing lineages, deep ancestors use a reconstruction with those lineages hidden.

    Lecio only reconstructs whether gene families were present or absent. It says nothing about DNA or RNA sequences or what organelles looked like.

    If a species is not in the data, search for its genus or family; Lecio shows the ancestors of the closest group it has.

    The number of LECA gene families shifted as species were added. The deeper the ancestor, the more carefully you should read B, C and D calls.
