# Bacterial and archaeal traits

`madin2020_condensed_species_NCBI.csv.gz`: Madin, J.S. et al. (2020) A synthesis of bacterial and
archaeal phenotypic trait data. *Scientific Data* 7:170. Species-level condensed table
(14,893 species, NCBI taxonomy): growth temperature, optimum temperature and pH, oxygen
metabolism, isolation source, genome size, GC, motility, cell shape, pathways, carbon substrates.
Downloaded from https://github.com/bacteria-archaea-traits/bacteria-archaea-traits
(output/condensed_species_NCBI.csv), licence CC BY 4.0. Values are literature compilations,
not measurements made here.
